Common Questions

FAQ

How quickly can you mobilise on a job?
Usually within the same week for smaller jobs. Larger projects requiring method statements and full documentation may take a little longer to prep, but we move fast. Call us if it's urgent.
Do you service buildings outside Auckland?
Our primary focus is the Auckland region. For larger projects in other parts of New Zealand, get in touch and we'll discuss options.
What information do you need to give me a quote?
Building height and type, the scope of work, any known access constraints, and your rough timeline. A site visit is often the best way to scope a job accurately.
Are you insured?
Yes. We hold public liability insurance and all required insurances for rope access work in New Zealand. Details available on request.
Do you provide H&S documentation?
Yes, method statements, risk assessments, and rescue plans are provided for every job as standard. We can also provide our H&S management plan and cert registers on request.
Can you work on occupied buildings?
Yes. Rope access is well-suited to occupied buildings, our small footprint and fast setup means minimal disruption. We can also work in out-of-hours windows where needed.
