How We Started

From the Rope Up

A-Z Access Solutions was founded by Zannah-Rose and Amin, bringing together more than 20 years of combined rope access experience across New Zealand. Over the years, we've worked on a wide variety of projects, including airport hangars, stadiums, high-rise buildings, industrial sites, and commercial properties.

Established in 2023 and based on Auckland's North Shore, we created A-Z Access Solutions to provide safe, practical, and reliable rope access services with a strong focus on quality workmanship and professional service. We believe that good communication, careful planning, and attention to detail are just as important as the work itself.

Our team includes IRATA-certified rope access technicians and SiteSafe passport holders, backed by comprehensive public liability insurance and a commitment to maintaining high standards of health and safety. Whether it's routine maintenance, inspections, installations, or specialist access work, we approach every project with the same goal: delivering safe, efficient, and dependable results for our clients.
