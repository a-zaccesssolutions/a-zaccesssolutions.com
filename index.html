What We Do

Rope Access & Building Maintenance

Building Washes & Window Cleans

Full building washes, window cleans, gutter cleans, and roof washes using multiple methodologies and a plan tailored to your property.

Painting & Coatings

Exterior painting, protective coatings, and surface treatments applied by certified technicians where access is difficult.

Sealant & Leak Repairs

Sealant replacement, caulking, and targeted leak repairs on facades, glazing joints, and penetrations, fixed cleanly at height.

Building Inspections & Reports

Detailed condition assessments covering facade integrity, glazing, cladding, and structural fixings with written reports.

Facade Remedials & Glazing

Facade remediation, glazing replacement, and cladding repairs with practical access planning and no unnecessary delays.

Construction Assistance

Rope access support for construction teams, including high-level installation help, facade assistance, and hard-to-reach tasks with the right access setup.

Banner Installation

Banner, signage, and promotional install support for high-level placements, awkward elevations, and short access windows.

Bird Proofing & Pest Control

Professional proofing and pest-control support for building exteriors, including deterrent systems installed safely via rope access.

Green Wall Maintenance

At-height green wall care, trimming, irrigation checks, plant health reviews, and maintenance access planning for vertical landscapes.

Property Maintenance Needs

Talk to us about your property maintenance needs and we can discuss the safest, cleanest options for your building.
