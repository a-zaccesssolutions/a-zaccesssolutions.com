Certification Standard

IRATA Levels Explained

L1
Technician

Entry-level certification. Technicians work under direct supervision of a Level 3. Competent in rope access fundamentals, ascending, descending, and basic rescue.

L2
Technician

Mid-level certification. Can work more independently, supervise Level 1 technicians, and conduct more complex rigging and rescue operations.

L3
Supervisor

Senior certification. Responsible for supervising rope access work, writing method statements, conducting rescue plans, and ensuring IRATA compliance across the team.
